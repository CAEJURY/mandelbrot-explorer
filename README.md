# Mandelbrot

A quiet explorer for the Mandelbrot set. The picture fills the window; the controls stay out of the way.

Drag to pan. Click to zoom in, shift-click or right-click to zoom out, scroll to zoom toward the pointer. Pick a coast — Seahorse, Elephant, Triple spiral, Minibrot — or reset the view. Iterations follow the zoom until you move the slider.

Live view: https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/CAEJURY/mandelbrot-explorer/blob/main/index.html
